# Midnight Star Chart Journal - 3am
## A Midnight Journel to write your midnight thoughts :). You can write your thoughts and it will be saved as a shining star in your journel, that can be viewed when clicked with date and time.

## Description:
### Its a simple journal website with dark theme made from vanilla HTML CSS and Javascript, where you can add thoughts in your journel with a title and description and it will be saved in localstorage that can be viewed later and can be deleted any time. I have also added shooting stars to make it look space theme.

## Why I made it?
### To write and save my reviews about 3am in the journal... It can also be used to write your life goals to save it and never look back again because you are just a overthinker -_- , I also made it to learn new Javascript codes. 

## How to save your thoughts?
### Its very simple to save your thoughts by just entering your thought title in the provided space for your title (Enter Your Title), and than add your thought description in the provided space for your thought (Enter your Midnight Thought), and than by clicking on submit star button your thought will be submitted and will be seen as a star in the free space, by clicking on the star you can see your thought details with the date and time when it was submited, and you can delete your thought by clicking on delete button or close it by clicking on cross sign.

## Inspiration
### A normal journal website, to improve my website dev skills.
